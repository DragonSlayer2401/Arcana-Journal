# Illustrated Rider–Waite–Smith deck

The user's uploaded artwork is a style reference, not a source of characters or a licensed asset for redistribution. The original reference stays in `art/references/style-reference.png` and is excluded from release archives and the deployed app.

Public-domain Rider–Waite–Smith card scans supply the scenes and symbolism. Every illustrated card is generated separately with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as a 768 × 1152 WebP for local/offline use. No image service runs in the app.

Visual direction: angular flat-color forms, flowing decorative curves, rich jewel tones, geometric fabric patterns, and a textured gouache finish. The generated Fool is the consistency anchor; each card retains its own scene and meaningful color associations.

Generation records and prompts are retained with the full-size outputs in `art/generated`. Source provenance is recorded in `art/references/sources.json`. The original user-supplied style collage is not granted the project's MIT license.
